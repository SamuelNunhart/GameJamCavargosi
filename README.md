# Doomscrolling Jam Concept Brief

## Working Title
**Just One More**

## Core Idea
The feed keeps intruding, offering useful information, but every interaction with it increases corruption and makes the room harder to trust.

## Main Mechanic
### Temptation vs Corruption
- The feed gives the player helpful info
- Interacting with it raises **Corruption**
- Higher corruption makes the room more distorted and unreliable

## Strong Supporting Mechanics
### 1. Memory Disruption
- The player sees useful information briefly
- Then must do a task that makes them forget
- They can re-check, but that increases corruption

### 2. Feed Pull / Attention Gravity
- The feed appears on screen and pulls the mouse/cursor toward it
- The player must resist
- A **Focus** button can reduce the pull temporarily

### 3. Trust / Doubt Signals
- The player receives clues from both the room and the feed
- The feed is loud and immediate, but not always fully trustworthy
- Real-world clues are quieter, but more reliable

## Example Gameplay Loop
1. Feed shows a useful clue (code, object order, correct item)
2. Player tries to remember it
3. During the next task, the feed interferes:
   - cursor pull
   - fake reminders
   - misleading prompts
4. Player chooses:
   - trust memory
   - trust room clues
   - re-check the feed and gain corruption

## Example Tasks
### Lamp
Identify the real switch among fake ones.

### Toothbrush
Remember which object is correct after a brief clue.

### Window
Hold interaction while resisting cursor pull and fake warnings.

### End
Final sequence where the player must ignore the strongest temptations.

## Win / Lose States
### Win
The player completes the routine without fully giving in.

### Lose
- Too much **Corruption**: the room becomes the feed
- Too much **Stress**: the player panics and grabs the phone

## Scope for 48 Hours
Keep it small:
- 1 room
- 4 short tasks
- 1 corruption meter
- 1 stress/focus mechanic
- 1 ending sequence

## One-Sentence Pitch
A short psychological horror game where you try to get ready for bed without doomscrolling, 
but the feed keeps tempting you with useful information while slowly corrupting reality.
