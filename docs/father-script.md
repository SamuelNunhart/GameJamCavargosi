# Father’s story

## Cutscene 01

*Father tries to fix the kitchen sink. The water won’t drain. He fights with it for a moment, then gives up and steps back.*

**FATHER:** Why won’t this thing drain?

**FATHER:** God damnit.

*Father goes to the fridge, opens it and gets a beer.*

> Gameplay note · This is his third beer. He drank the first two before the cutscene.

**FATHER:** Ahh, that’s better.

*He returns to the sink, but the water is still sitting in the basin.*

> Gameplay note · After the second beer, the edges of the screen blur slightly. After the third, the blur deepens and the view starts to sway.

> Production note · Decide what he does at the sink once the models and minigame are ready.

**FATHER:** Damned sink! Screw this! It’s late anyway. I should get some sleep. I’ll get a shot to sleep better.

*Father opens the fridge again and takes out a bottle of vodka and a cup.*

**FATHER:** No more than two shots…

*He fills up the cup a bit more than he wanted. He gulps it down.*

> Gameplay note · At the equivalent of four beers or more, the room starts to spin and his vision blurs a little more. The effect lasts a moment and leads into the blackout.

**FATHER:** That really… hit… the spot…

*He tries to go to bed, but he falls down on the couch and falls asleep there.*

*During the night, the ghost approaches him.*

**GHOST:** You never did get that sink to drain.

**FATHER:** What…? WHO ARE YOU?! GET OUT OF MY HOUSE!

**GHOST:** I cannot leave. Not yet. I’m here to help you, Robert.

**FATHER:** HOW DO YOU KNOW MY NAME? No, I don’t care! You know what? I’m calling the police!

*Father tries to grab his phone, but his vision is still far from sharp. He tries to enter his PIN. He fails on the first try.*

**FATHER:** You can’t be serious!

*He fails the second attempt. His head starts to hurt.*

*Before he can make the third attempt, the phone slips from his hands.*

**FATHER:** Shit!

**GHOST:** If I were a real threat, you and your family would be in danger because of you.

**FATHER:** I was trying to protect them. I just dropped the damn phone.

**GHOST:** You’d already gotten the PIN wrong twice, Robert. You’re too drunk to call for help.

**GHOST:** Why are you doing this to them? To yourself?

**FATHER:** I’m not doing anything wrong!

**GHOST:** So you don’t think drinking every day is wrong?

**FATHER:** A few beers never hurt anybody.

**GHOST:** Is that what you tell yourself?

**FATHER:** It’s just a few beers! Unlike what she is doing!

**GHOST:** She? You mean your wife?

**FATHER:** You see anyone else around here feeding the slot machines with our money? I sure as hell don’t!

**GHOST:** I’m sorry to hear that. However, tonight, I’m here because of you.

**FATHER:** Are you bloody serious? She—

**GHOST:** Don’t worry. She’s next on my list. I promise to pay her a visit as well.

**FATHER:** All right? What do you want from me?!

**GHOST:** I want you to take care of your family just like you used to. Not right now. But tomorrow morning, you should take care of the house, without alcohol, of course.

**FATHER:** Wait, so I can’t drink?

**GHOST:** You can. But once you start, you know you have a hard time stopping.

**FATHER:** Bullshit. I can stop whenever I like.

**GHOST:** I’ll let you sleep, but don’t forget what I have just told you. When you wake up, I’ll be around, watching.

**FATHER:** You’re a real weirdo, you know that?

**GHOST:** Sweet dreams, Robert.

**FATHER:** Whatever…

*Father drifts to sleep.*

## Cutscene 02

*Father wakes up the next day. The whole family is already gone – off to work or school.*

**FATHER:** I don’t need to rush this. If I don’t get it done by Sunday, there’s always next week anyway.

**GHOST:** Putting work off again, aren’t we?

**FATHER:** Ugh! You’re still here?!

**GHOST:** I told you I’d stick around. You said that last week, Robert. Sunday’s four days away. If you keep putting it off, that sink will still be clogged next Sunday.

**FATHER:** …

**FATHER:** …I guess I could give the sink another try.

> Gameplay note · Control returns to the player.

> [The dishes unlock on a later day once the sink can drain, even if the repair was imperfect.]

> [The checklist on the fridge shows task status and marks completed tasks.]

## Cutscene 03 – Day 02

> [Planned follow-up task: make dinner. The requirements for unlocking it are still open.]

> [If the sink is still clogged after a repair attempt or blackout, Father can retry it on a later day. The dishes stay locked.]

> [Father can still drink. A blackout costs a day, and any unfinished task remains available to retry.]

## Cutscene 04 – Day 03

*The lights go out at the start of the third day.*

> Gameplay note · Planned · The outage makes the power repair minigame available. The affected room stays dark if the power is left unrepaired. The outage happens on Day 3 even if Father lost an earlier day to a blackout. With one main task per day, a lost day leaves too little time to finish every task by Sunday.
